POP Ceiling in Tathawade, Pune: Coordinate Details Room by Room

A decorative POP detail should support how each room is used. For a Tathawade project, make a separate ceiling plan for each room and coordinate its profile, lights, fan and finished level before finalising the design.

Separate the room plans and transitions

Draw each room with its dimensions, doors, windows, furniture heights and fixed fittings. Mark where a profile belongs and where a plain surface is preferable. If a ceiling design crosses a doorway, choose a deliberate transition rather than carrying a decorative line through by default.

For an open living and dining area, define whether the ceiling uses one continuous level or separate zones. Show the line between those zones on plan and section, and check it against the furniture layout, fan position and any curtain track.

Quick check

  • Individual room plans measured
  • Plain and decorative zones marked
  • Doorway transitions resolved

Coordinate the light and fan positions

Overlay the light schedule and fan centre on each ceiling drawing. Mark the actual products, cut-outs and switching requirements. Ask the electrician to confirm the wiring and fitting details, while the ceiling installer confirms that the profile and support arrangement accommodates them.

Review the ceiling in both day and evening conditions if possible. Confirm that the planned light source is not blocked by a ledge and that replaceable parts remain reachable. Any driver or connection that requires service should have a recorded access route.

Quick check

  • Light models and fan centres shown
  • Electrician reviewed the wiring plan
  • Replaceable components remain accessible

Keep finish expectations consistent

Agree the profile sample, corner treatment, joint finish and painting scope for each room. Ask how differences in measured dimensions will affect the final design. Save the approved drawings with the estimate and record any site change before it is completed.
