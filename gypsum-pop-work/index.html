Frequently Asked Questions About Gypsum & POP Work in Pune

1. What is the difference between a gypsum board ceiling and traditional POP work?

A gypsum board ceiling uses factory-manufactured boards mounted on a galvanized iron frame, ensuring a smooth, crack-free finish with fast installation. Traditional POP work uses wet Plaster of Paris applied over chicken mesh, which allows for handmade ornamental shapes but requires longer drying times.

2. How is wall molding design executed in modern Pune apartments?

Wall molding design uses lightweight pre-formed polymer, PU, or POP strips affixed to walls in rectangular or geometric panel frames (wainscoting). Once painted with royale silk emulsions, it gives rooms a classic, luxurious European aesthetic.

3. Can POP wall design be customized for TV unit backdrops?

Yes! A specialized pop wall design or 3D relief paneling is frequently used behind TV entertainment centers to create striking architectural depth, often paired with hidden LED profile lighting channels.

4. Do your gypsum and POP services include crack-resistant joint finishing?

Absolutely. We use specialized joint paper tapes, fiberglass mesh, and multi-layer acrylic joint compounds to completely eliminate hairline cracks at board joints before the final Painting stage.
