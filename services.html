Our Core Expertise

Master-Class Execution

We don't juggle a hundred things. We focus strictly on Ceilings, Electricals, and Painting to deliver absolute structural perfection and aesthetic brilliance for clients across Pune.

1. False Ceiling & POP Frameworks

The foundation of a luxury interior starts from the top. We engineer ceiling structures that guarantee zero sagging, thermal resistance, and flawless alignment.

Gyproc Expert Material

We highly recommend and utilize authentic Saint-Gobain Gyproc boards. Mounted on heavy-gauge metal channels, this system prevents hairline cracks and handles heavy chandelier loads with ease.

Regular False Ceiling

A cost-effective yet structurally sound standard ceiling solution. Perfect for large carpet areas where clean leveling and minimal lighting integrations are required.

Wall Moulding & POP

Bring Parisian elegance to your living spaces with our intricate wall mouldings, cornices, and fluted panels. We offer complete transparency with highly skilled POP labor starting at ₹13 per sq. ft.

Gypsum Partitions

Heavy-duty, sound-insulated drywall partitions. Ideal for quickly dividing office cabins in commercial spaces or creating walk-in wardrobes in residential villas.

Grid Ceilings

Acoustical grid panels perfectly suited for corporate offices, retail showrooms, and commercial spaces across Hinjawadi and Kharadi IT parks.

Acrylic & PVC/WPC

100% moisture-resistant and termite-proof ceiling solutions. Expertly installed in bathrooms, outdoor balconies, and high-humidity areas to ensure lifelong durability.

2. Advanced Electrical & Lighting

A premium ceiling demands superior lighting logic. We handle turnkey electrical overhauls ensuring safety, heavy appliance load balancing, and stunning ambient illumination.

Concealed Wiring Networks

Complete removal of old wires and installation of fire-retardant, high-grade concealed wiring. We design robust conduit layouts that hide perfectly within the architectural framework.

Ambient LED & Profile Setup

Precision routing for magnetic track lights, cove lighting, and seamless LED profiles. We synchronize the electrical layout exactly with the false ceiling blueprint for zero rework.

3. Professional Painting

The final finish defines the luxury of a space. We enforce a rigorous surface preparation protocol before a single drop of paint touches your walls.

Surface Preparation

Application of double-layer premium wall putty followed by mechanized sanding to create an ultra-smooth, flawless canvas.

Anti-Fungal Priming

Sealing the walls with high-grade primers to block moisture and ensure the final paint layers bond perfectly without peeling.

Luxury Emulsions

Expert application of high-end washable emulsions (Asian Paints Royale, Jotun) and custom textured accent walls for a sophisticated finish.
