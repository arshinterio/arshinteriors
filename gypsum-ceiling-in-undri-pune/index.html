Gypsum Ceiling in Undri, Pune: Review the Paint-Ready Handover

A paint-ready handover avoids confusion between the ceiling installer and painter. If you are planning gypsum ceiling work in Undri, make the inspection stage and final touch-up responsibility part of the written scope.

Define what 'ready for paint' means

Ask the ceiling installer to state which joints, fasteners and edges will be treated and what surface condition is handed to the painter. Confirm whether any additional primer, filling or sanding is required, and who performs it. A shared sample or written finish description can reduce differing expectations.

Inspect the ceiling in the actual room under diffuse light and the planned fittings where possible. Look for an even plane, clean corners and accurately formed openings. Record items that need correction before the painter starts.

Quick check

  • Handover condition described in writing
  • Inspection occurs before paint
  • Corrections recorded and assigned

Protect details and coordinate cut-outs

Confirm each opening against the selected light or fan model. Ask who provides the final cut-out measurements and who repairs an opening if a fitting changes. Keep the electrician involved in wiring and support decisions for the actual installation.

Protect finished edges and surfaces during later work. If carpentry or other trades still need to access the ceiling, agree how they will do so without damaging the paint-ready surface.

Quick check

  • Cut-outs checked against product dimensions
  • Change responsibility assigned
  • Surface protection plan agreed

Keep the approved scope with the estimate

A complete estimate identifies board and framing, joint treatment, paint, cut-outs, access, protection and clean-up. Note whether the amount assumes measured dimensions and how additions are approved.

  • Inclusions and exclusions clear
  • Final dimensions and changes documented
