Grid Ceiling in Hinjawadi, Pune: Coordinate Office Services

Office ceilings often need to coordinate several services in one overhead zone. For a grid ceiling project in Hinjawadi, issue one reflected ceiling plan that aligns the tile module with each service and its future access needs.

Start with a coordinated reflected ceiling plan

Measure the finished space and locate walls, columns, partitions and bulkheads. Set out grid lines and show boundary tiles. Overlay the furniture or workstation plan so lights and air outlets can be considered against the areas they are meant to serve.

List all overhead services before ordering the grid: lighting, diffusers, detectors, speakers, cable trays, access points and any other equipment in the project scope. Each trade should confirm its own product dimensions, clearances and support requirements against the chosen ceiling system.

Quick check

  • Room and partition plan verified
  • Every overhead service listed
  • Grid and service layout reviewed together

Keep inspection routes usable

Mark which modules must be removable to reach valves, connections or other maintainable services. Confirm the selected tile and grid arrangement permits the intended access, and label those locations on the handover drawing. An access tile is useful only if it is not blocked by fixed equipment or furniture.

Ask the electrical, ventilation and other service contractors to show their test or maintenance route. If a service changes position during installation, revise the ceiling plan before closing the area. Retain a final marked plan with the actual installed access points.

Quick check

  • Access tiles aligned to real service points
  • Equipment does not obstruct tile removal
  • As-installed plan retained

Check fixture support and tile specification

Record the tile product, module, finish and any acoustic or cleaning information relevant to the chosen system. Verify fixture compatibility and support details in the manufacturer's instructions; heavy equipment may require a support independent of the grid, depending on the system and site.
