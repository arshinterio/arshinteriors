Living-Room Wall Moulding Layout in Pune: Plan Around Openings and Furniture

Wall mouldings feel intentional when their scale relates to the wall and furniture. Before selecting a profile, make an elevation that includes doors, switches, curtains and the pieces that will occupy the room.

Measure the wall that will be visible

Record wall width and height, then mark doors, windows, skirting, switches and wall-mounted equipment. The remaining visible area is the real design canvas; an empty rectangle can lead to awkward interruptions.

Photograph the wall straight on and note whether the profile continues around a corner. Decide where the feature starts and ends before laying out panels.

Quick check

  • Openings and fixed points measured
  • Corner transitions drawn

Set panel proportions and profile scale

Compare panel widths, vertical spacing and margins on the measured elevation. Try a small number of layouts and keep boundaries clear of switches; check the arrangement with the sofa or console in place.

Review an actual profile sample or full-scale corner mock-up. A small catalogue picture can hide the depth and shadow. Agree the corner joints and stop points before installation.

Quick check

  • Panel spacing checked against elevation
  • Profile depth and corners reviewed

Coordinate wall preparation and paint

Confirm preparation before fixing and how joints are filled. Record whether the profile and wall share a finish or use contrast, then include paint product and sheen assumptions.

Frequently asked questions

Should moulding panels align with a sofa?

Review the layout with furniture positions, but use an elevation to judge visual balance rather than forcing every line to match.

Can moulding be fixed to an uneven wall?

The installer should inspect the surface and discuss preparation; the method depends on the wall and selected profile.
