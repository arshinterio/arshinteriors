Ask how the board layout and frame are specified

Request a drawing or written description of the ceiling frame, board type, fixing approach and any perimeter detail. These choices should follow the system supplier's instructions and the site conditions. If the ceiling crosses a beam or changes level, ask for that junction to be shown rather than left to improvisation.

The board pattern and framing determine where joints fall and where fittings can be placed. Mark cut-outs and heavier fixtures early, and have the responsible installer identify any separate support needed. Do not rely on the finished paint layer to hide a poorly coordinated joint or opening.

Quick check

  • Frame and board specification recorded
  • Changes in level and beam junctions drawn
  • Fixture support responsibility identified

Review joint treatment under suitable light

Ask the installer to explain the joint-filling and surface preparation sequence before paint. Look for an even plane, neat internal and external corners, clean cut-outs and consistent transitions. Inspect the ceiling with daylight and the planned lights where possible; grazing light can reveal ridges that are hard to see from directly below.

Discuss the final finish and touch-up responsibility in advance. Keep an agreed sample or reference for colour and sheen. If another contractor will paint, make sure the ceiling is handed over at the agreed preparation stage and that the painter knows which areas need primer or additional preparation.

Quick check

  • Surface inspected from several angles
  • Cut-outs and corners checked
  • Painting handover stage defined

Keep a photo record before closing access

Photograph the ceiling routes and any planned service access before boards conceal the frame. Store those images with the final plan so future maintenance does not depend on memory. If access panels are required, mark their exact positions, dimensions and finish before painting.

Before booking, ask the gypsum ceiling contractor to identify the board and framing specification, joint treatment, cut-outs and the surface handover stage in writing.
