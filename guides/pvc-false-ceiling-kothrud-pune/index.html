Check the panel and fixing specification

Ask the installer to identify the panel product, dimensions, finish and installation guidance in the written scope. Review how panels meet at edges, corners and wall junctions, and whether the selected trim is included. Keep the product information with the estimate so the agreed material is clear.

Do not assume all PVC panels have the same performance or are suitable for every room. Check the manufacturer's care and installation instructions against the actual site conditions, including heat, moisture and any ventilation requirements.

Quick check

  • Product and finish named in writing
  • Panel joints, trims and perimeter shown
  • Care and installation instructions reviewed

Coordinate lights and future access

Mark each light and fan position on the ceiling plan before fixing the panel layout. Confirm cut-out sizes against the chosen fixture instructions and have the responsible tradesperson plan wiring, support and safe connections for the specific site.

Ask how a panel or fitting can be reached if a connection needs inspection later. Record the access method and clarify how changes to fixture positions affect the panel layout and estimate.

Quick check

  • Fixture positions and cut-outs coordinated
  • Wiring and supports assigned to the responsible trade
  • Maintenance access discussed
