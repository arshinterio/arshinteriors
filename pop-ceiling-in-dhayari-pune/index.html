Set the POP profile against the room dimensions

A reference photo may show the style, but the contractor still needs a measured plan for the actual room. Decide whether you want a simple perimeter profile, a centre feature or a transition between ceiling levels. Show the start and end points, corner treatment, beam junctions and curtain-track clearance.

Ask for a small sample or a section drawing when the profile has a projection or a change in level. Review how it meets the wall and how the finished line will look from the room entrance.

Quick check

  • Reference translated to a measured layout
  • Corners and stop points drawn
  • Wall and curtain-track junction reviewed

Coordinate the jhoomar, cove and electrical points

Mark the central jhoomar or other main fitting on the plan before POP work begins. Confirm the fixture's weight and support method with the responsible installer, and coordinate the wiring route with the electrician. Position smaller spotlights only after checking their product dimensions and fitting requirements.

For a concealed cove light, ask to see a section with the cove depth, light position and driver access. Keep the installation and electrical work in the responsibility of the qualified tradespeople handling the project.

Quick check

  • Central fitting point and support responsibility clear
  • Light cut-outs coordinated with the POP layout
  • Driver and wiring access considered

Agree on surface finish and paint handover

Before approval, write down the POP areas, surface preparation, joint and edge finish, primer or paint responsibility and cleanup. Inspect the completed profile from several angles and under the room's intended lighting; uneven edges or rough transitions are easier to address before the finish is accepted.
