Comprehensive False Ceiling Solutions in Pune

A well-designed false ceiling does more than just conceal wiring—it defines the architectural character, improves thermal insulation, and allows for layered mood lighting in your home or office. As a leading **false ceiling contractor in Pune**, Arsh Interiors specializes in executing flawless designs tailored to modern Indian apartments and commercial establishments.

Types of False Ceilings We Install Across Pune:

  • Gypsum Board False Ceiling: Lightweight, seamless, and fire-resistant. Perfect for living rooms, bedrooms, and false ceiling designs with cove lighting in Wakad, Baner, and Kharadi.
  • POP (Plaster of Paris) Ceilings: Traditional and highly customizable for intricate traditional or contemporary molding patterns.
  • Waterproof PVC Ceiling Panels: Ideal for bathrooms, kitchens, balconies, and commercial shops needing moisture-resistant overhead finishes.
  • Suspended Grid Ceiling (T-Grid): Commercial-grade acoustic tile ceilings widely installed in offices, retail stores, and corporate spaces across Pune.

Executing a false ceiling requires strict alignment, durable channels (like Saint-Gobain Gyproc or equivalent high-grade frameworks), and expert finishing. Based in Dhayari, Pune, our team manages end-to-end execution—from framing and electrical ducting layout to final putty and paint finishing.
