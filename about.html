About Arsh Interiors

Based in Dhayari, Pune, Arsh Interiors (Arsh Interior) is dedicated to delivering uncompromised quality in interior execution, false ceiling contracting, and complete home transformations.

Built on Trust and Precision Execution

At Arsh Interiors, we believe that brilliant design deserves flawless execution. Founded and operated with a hands-on approach to contracting, our team brings years of specialized expertise in Gypsum false ceilings, POP designs, wall mouldings, concealed electrical wiring, and luxury wall painting across Pune.

Whether you are setting up a new 2 BHK / 3 BHK apartment in Wakad, Baner, or Kharadi, or renovating a commercial workspace, we eliminate middleman hassles by providing direct contracting transparency, strict timeline adherence, and premium materials sourced directly from authorized manufacturers like Saint-Gobain Gyproc.

Our Mission

To deliver transparent, reliable, and high-standard interior contracting services with absolute structural durability and zero hidden costs.

Our Commitment

Direct supervision on every project site in Pune, ensuring precise measurements, neat finishing, and on-time handover.
